01 / PROJECT SNAPSHOT

A REDESIGN EXPLORATION,
NOT A CLAIM OF MEASURED RESULTS.

02

Let the brand
set the mood.

Sidra sells scent diffusers and oils for homes, offices and hospitality spaces. The redesign should feel calm and distinctive while helping visitors understand the products and move confidently toward a choice.

Design opportunity
Translate an atmospheric brand into a practical ecommerce journey without flattening its character into a standard shop template.

Scope
Homepage storytelling, category structure, product browsing, shopping states and a visual system to carry them across screens.

Evidence
Public website captures from 26 October 2026. A design exploration, with no measured conversion or usability results.

02 / DESIGN ANALYSIS

FROM PRODUCT SPECS
TO A SENSE OF PLACE.

B / 01

Atmosphere with purpose

A deep forest canvas, warm clay accents and a Georgia serif set the mood before any product appears. The hero carries one promise and one action, so the atmosphere frames the shop instead of competing with it.

SEE / 03 BEFORE & AFTER · 01
B / 02

Curated discovery

Three need-based entry points and three picks by space (home, office, hospitality) give visitors a way in before they meet the full catalogue. Labels describe what a product does for a room, not only its model number.

SEE / 03 BEFORE & AFTER · 02
B / 03

Shopping states

Search, a price filter, sorting, category pills, product badges, wishlist and basket feedback support the move from browsing to choosing. These interactions are front-end demonstrations and need real commerce back-end before launch.

SEE / LIVE SITE · SHOP + BASKET
B / 04

Consistency across screens

The same colours, type pair and radius scale run from the homepage into the shop and account screens, so secondary tasks still feel like Sidra while staying readable.

01 / Homepage & brand atmosphereORIGINAL ↔ REDESIGN
ANALYSIS / WHAT CHANGED

The original homepage leads with product imagery and store navigation. The redesign opens on a dark forest canvas with one serif promise, a short line of context and a single route into the collection, so the brand mood and the shopping start point arrive together.

02 / Category & product discoveryORIGINAL ↔ REDESIGN
ANALYSIS / WHAT CHANGED

The original category page lists diffusers with technical detail first. The redesign groups products by need and by space, adds category pills and badges, and keeps specifications such as capacity and coverage in plain Inter so they stay easy to compare.

05 / REFLECTION

ATMOSPHERE IS USEFUL
WHEN IT HELPS PEOPLE CHOOSE.

Make room for
meaning.

The redesign keeps Sidra’s products and specifications, but lets the brand set the mood first and then narrows the choice by need and by space, so atmosphere and usability work in the same direction.

WHAT TO VALIDATE NEXT

Test whether visitors can find the right diffuser for their space, use filters and sorting with confidence, and understand basket feedback. Search, wishlist and basket are front-end concepts and need real commerce support before launch.
