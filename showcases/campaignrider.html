01 / PROJECT SNAPSHOT

CAMPAIGN GROWTH MEETS
BUSINESS TECHNOLOGY.

03

A broader story.
A clearer journey.

CampaignRider spans email delivery, CRM, cloud, security, and digital services. The redesign brings these capabilities into one connected visual story.

Design opportunity
Retain the recognizable yellow, blue and dark typography while replacing disconnected content blocks with a more deliberate hierarchy and consistent illustration language.

Scope
Homepage art direction, navigation, service presentation, illustration system, and pathways to contact.

Evidence
Public website captures from 24 October 2026. A design exploration, with no measured conversion or usability results.

02 / DESIGN ANALYSIS

FROM SEPARATE SERVICES
TO A CONNECTED SYSTEM.

C / 01

A connected proposition

The original introduces business technology through a text-led hero. The redesign pairs an oversized message with dimensional technology artwork and a Reach → Engage → Connect → Grow journey, giving the breadth of the offer a visible structure.

SEE / 03 BEFORE & AFTER · 01
C / 02

A consistent illustration language

The original mixes email character imagery, a Zoho application diagram and partner logos. The redesign connects the hero, email and integration stories through blue, yellow and white dimensional illustrations while retaining partner identities.

SEE / LIVE SITE · EMAIL + INTEGRATIONS
C / 03

A deliberate content rhythm

Alternating text and imagery, a contained integration panel and rounded service cards distinguish the main content groups. Familiar service names remain, while hierarchy, spacing and presentation change.

SEE / 03 BEFORE & AFTER · 02
C / 04

A focused next step

The original ends with a contact form on the homepage. The redesign separates the final invitation from the form, directing visitors to a dedicated contact page. The extra navigation step is a trade-off to validate.

01 / Homepage & first impressionORIGINAL ↔ REDESIGN
ANALYSIS / WHAT CHANGED

The original uses a wide text-led introduction with two actions. The redesign adds technology artwork, stronger type contrast and a linked customer journey beneath the hero. Yellow remains the primary action cue.

02 / Core service discoveryORIGINAL ↔ REDESIGN
ANALYSIS / WHAT CHANGED

Both versions present the same eight core service categories. The redesign uses rounded cards, contained logo areas and consistent action placement. This is a visual and hierarchy change; improved task completion remains to be tested.

04 / VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM

THE TOKENS BEHIND
THE REDESIGN.

Gold moves.

WHAT TO VALIDATE NEXT
