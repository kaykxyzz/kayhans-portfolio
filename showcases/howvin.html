01 / PROJECT SNAPSHOT

A REDESIGN EXPLORATION,
NOT A CLAIM OF MEASURED RESULTS.

01

Make the next step
easier to see.

Howvin sells to hotels, resorts and restaurants as much as to homeowners. The redesign treats the site as both a product catalogue and a tool for people planning real spaces.

Design opportunity
Help visitors move from broad inspiration to useful product details and project-specific actions without losing the visual context.

Scope
Navigation structure, collection discovery, product cards, project and inquiry pathways, and a visual system to hold them together.

Evidence
Public website captures from 26 October 2026. A design exploration, with no measured conversion or usability results.

02 / DESIGN ANALYSIS

FROM COMPANY STRUCTURE
TO BUYER INTENT.

A / 01

Navigation by intent

The original menu follows company structure: Home, About us, Products, Project, News, Contact us and Blog, with 11 product genres and 8 project categories behind dropdowns. The redesign leads with how buyers think (Spaces, Collections, Projects, FAQ, Shop, Blog, About) and opens with six settings: hotels, resorts, restaurants, beach clubs, villas and rooftops.

SEE / 03 BEFORE & AFTER · 01
A / 02

Discovery to shortlist

All eleven genres sit in one row of filter pills with a sort control. Each card shows a category badge, the product name, material tags and a “+ Inquiry” button, so a buyer can build a shortlist without leaving the grid. A counter in the header keeps that shortlist visible.

SEE / 03 BEFORE & AFTER · 02
A / 03

A project-oriented workflow

Projects filter by the same eight categories Howvin already publishes. A five-step process (Consult, Select, Customise, Produce, Support) sets expectations, and the inquiry form asks only for name, email, company and project notes. The inquiry list, login and language switch are front-end concepts.

SEE / LIVE SITE · PROJECTS + INQUIRY
A / 04

Visual discipline

A light Fraunces serif, Hanken Grotesk, one teal for actions and pill-shaped controls replace the original mix of all-caps headlines and slider overlays. Photography carries the colour, so the interface can stay quiet.

01 / Homepage & navigationORIGINAL ↔ REDESIGN
ANALYSIS / WHAT CHANGED

The original opens on a carousel slide, “Howvin’s product advantages”, with four feature blurbs set over the photo and a floating WhatsApp, e-mail, WeChat and Kimi bar. The redesign uses one promise, “Outdoor living, considered.”, a short description and two clear actions: explore collections or discuss a project. The header adds an inquiry counter, a three-language switch and dark mode.

02 / Catalogue & discoveryORIGINAL ↔ REDESIGN
ANALYSIS / WHAT CHANGED

The original listing shows four cards per row with truncated titles, dense descriptions and a checkbox-style “Add to Inquiry”. The redesign puts the eleven genres and a sort control above the grid, adds category badges and material tags to each card, and turns inquiry into a clear “+ Inquiry” button next to the title.

05 / REFLECTION

MAKE THE REASONING
VISIBLE.

Clarity is a
design decision.

The redesign keeps Howvin’s catalogue, projects and contact details, but changes the order a visitor meets them in: a clear promise first, then spaces, then products, then real installations, then a simple way to start a project.

WHAT TO VALIDATE NEXT

Test whether trade buyers can find a product genre, shortlist several pieces and send an inquiry without help. The inquiry list, login and language switch are front-end concepts and need real back-end support before launch.
